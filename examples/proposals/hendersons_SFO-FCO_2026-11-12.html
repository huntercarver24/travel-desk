What happens next

  1. Reply with the option you'd like, or call us with questions.
  2. We schedule a 15-minute call. On the call, we confirm the seats are still open, then you move the points from your Chase account while we're on the line (transfers are instant for Air France).
  3. We book the seats in your Flying Blue account and pay the taxes on the card you choose.
  4. You receive the confirmation the same day, and we keep watching the booking for schedule changes and better seats.

Award booking service: $150 per ticket, credited back if you book a cash fare through us.

Good to know

  • Award seats and fares change daily. Everything here is based on availability and fares as of November 12, 2026, and is confirmed again before any points are transferred.
  • Taxes and fees are estimates until the ticket is issued.
  • Points transfers from a credit card to an airline cannot be reversed, which is why we confirm seats first.
  • Points and miles are held in your own accounts; we never ask for your passwords.
